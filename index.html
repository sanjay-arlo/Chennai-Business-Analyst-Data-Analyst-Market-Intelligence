How to interpret this project: the dataset is synthetic/illustrative. It demonstrates the Business Analyst process—defining questions, validating data, calculating KPIs, segmenting the sample, comparing roles and turning findings into decisions. It is not live Chennai hiring intelligence.

5. Plain-English Glossary

Salary midpointThe middle value between the listed minimum and maximum salary.
Skill demandThe percentage of sample listings that mention a skill at least once.
Work styleWhether a listing is hybrid, on-site or remote in the sample.
Hiring areaThe Chennai location recorded for the sample listing.
BA vs DA profileA comparison of which skills appear more often in the two role types.
Illustrative datasetData created to demonstrate analysis. It should not be presented as real market evidence.
