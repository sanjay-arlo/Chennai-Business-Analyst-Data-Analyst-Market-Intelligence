5. Plain-English Glossary

Salary midpointThe middle value between a listing's minimum and maximum salary.
Skill demandThe share of listings that mention a skill at least once.
Work styleHybrid, on-site or remote in the sample.
Hiring areaThe Chennai location recorded for the sample listing.
BA vs DA profileA comparison of which skills appear more often in each role.
Illustrative datasetPortfolio demonstration data, not live labour-market evidence.
